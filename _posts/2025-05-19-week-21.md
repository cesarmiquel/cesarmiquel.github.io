# Adlib Tracker 2

I use Adlib Tracker 2 (at2) to understand what type of music can be generated by the OPL2 chip. At2 is great but if you try to look at the keybindings is a little but overwhelming. In here I will do a little summary of the basic usage of AT2.

- With **F3**  you can load the module
- With **F5** you can play the whole song and **F6** to pause and **F7** to stop.
- With **F9** you can play the current pattern
- With **CTRL+ENTER** you can go to the next pattern
- In the main screen you can use **SPACE** bar to step through the pattern.
- **CTRL-P** opens the pattern list
- **CTRL-I** while playing, you can see the instrument list and see which instruments are activated by the pattern.
- To silence certain channels you can use **ALT 1...9** for (channels 1-9) and **ALT+SHIFT 0...9** for (channels 10-19)
- **ALT-R** will reset the channels
- **F1** can be used to see the help screen on any screen

## Main screen

F1 - Help

F2 - Save
F3 - Load

F5 - Play
F6 - Pause
F7 - Stop
F9 - Play cur pattern

[ENTER] - Ir al pattern order 

CTRL-P - Pattern list
CTRL-I - Instrument List

## Pattern editor

[SPACE] - Avanzar de un paso.
CTRL+ENTER      - Next pattern

### Toggle channels
ALT 1...9       - Toggle track channel
ALT+SHIFT 1...9 - Toggle track channel 1x
ALT+R           - Reset
