# One ROM

I found out about this project. It's awesome: basically it's simple circuitboard that replaces any ROM by a **Rasperrby Pi RP2350**. It can be used to replace the ROM of retro computers (24, 28, 32 and 40 pin roms). An interesting fact is that, for a C64, a single circuit can replace the 3 ROMS in the Commodore!

- [Web](https://onerom.org) - "One ROM is the most flexible ROM replacement for retro computers. It is based on a sub-$2 microcontroller and all of its software and hardware is completely open source. Boards can be fabbed for under $10 each in small quantities, or under $5 each in larger quantities. One ROM emulates the 24, 28, 32 and 40 pin EPROMs and mask programmed ROMs used in nearly every computer-based system from the late 1970s through to the early 1990s.""
- [Shop](https://shop.piers.rocks/product/one-rom/) - You can buy them here.
- [GH](https://github.com/piersfinlayson/one-rom) - Github page with source.
