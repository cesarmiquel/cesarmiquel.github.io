# Apple 2c Serial Port

I'm investigating how to make an RS-232 <-> MIDI bridge. I think it's quite simple. I want to connect my Apple 2c serial port to a MIDI device using an Arduino as a bridge. To test this I am analyzing (and checking the timming of my Apple 2c serial port since I know some old Apple 2c's, like mine, might have timming issues). To test it I'm attaching my logic analyzer to the serial port and do some tests. Here are a couple important links:

- [Serial port communication on Apple IIc.](https://c64retr.blogspot.com/2013/11/serial-port-communication-on-apple-iic.html) - This article in **c64retr.blogspot.com** explains how to configure and send data through the RS-232 ports on the Apple 2c. Since some blog posts tend to sometimes disappear I cloned the article here as a [Github gist](https://gist.github.com/cesarmiquel/8e7a8e8e67d599e45ed4d0c684b65dfb).
- [Most common baud rates table](https://lucidar.me/en/serialib/most-used-baud-rates-table/) - This article shows you the typical timing of RS-232 for different baud rates. The blog has many other interesting resources. Here is the table taken from there:
